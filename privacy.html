Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

J.A.C.K. is a personal desktop application used only by its owner. It is not distributed to other people.

What data the app uses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app reads the owner's own Google Calendar, Gmail, contacts and spreadsheets, only to show them inside the app.

Where the data stays

All data is processed and stored locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on that computer.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API data

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
